Heating
Draft — confirm with David

The radiators run from a thermostat in the hallway. Turn it up and give it twenty minutes.

The View is stone and it holds heat rather than grabbing it. It takes a little while to come round, and then it stays comfortable for hours without you touching anything.

If a radiator stays cold when the others are warm, let us know rather than wrestling with it — there is a trick to a couple of them.
