The View is a heritage building and it has its opinions. The sash windows need a firm hand rather than a gentle one — lift from the bottom rail, evenly.

The stairs creak. That is the building, not a fault.

Sound carries a little between rooms, so we would ask you to be kind to your neighbours after eleven.
