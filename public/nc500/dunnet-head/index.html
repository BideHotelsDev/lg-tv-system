Dunnet Head

Not John o’ Groats — Dunnet Head is the actual most northerly point of mainland Britain, and it is the better of the two by a distance.

Cliffs full of seabirds through the summer, a working lighthouse, and on a clear day the whole of Orkney laid out in front of you across the Firth.

Go in the evening if you can. The light coming off the water is the thing people remember.
